# Helios Knowledge Base (for evaluation)

## Free Trial
Every new account includes a 14-day free trial of Helios Compute with up to 2 virtual servers. No credit card is required to start the trial. When the trial ends the account pauses until a payment method is added, and trial data is retained for 30 days.

## Refunds
Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of their first paid invoice. Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 10 business days. After 30 days only prorated credits are offered, applied to the next billing cycle.

## Uptime SLA
Helios Compute guarantees 99.9 percent monthly uptime. Uptime between 99.0 and 99.9 percent earns a 10 percent service credit; below 99.0 percent earns a 25 percent credit. Credits must be claimed within 30 days of the incident.

## Support Tiers
Basic support is free with a two business day response. Standard support is 99 dollars per month with a four business hour response. Premium support is 499 dollars per month with a one hour response and a dedicated technical account manager.

## Data Regions
Helios Store data can live in North America, Europe, or Asia Pacific and never leaves the chosen region. Premium customers can negotiate custom data residency.

## Security
Data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3. Helios is SOC 2 Type II certified and supports two-factor authentication and SAML single sign-on. Access logs are kept for 12 months.

## Billing
Invoices are issued on the first of each month and are due within 15 days. Accounts over 30 days overdue are suspended. Annual prepayment receives a 15 percent discount.

## Error E-4021
Error E-4021 means a virtual server failed to attach its boot volume within 60 seconds. Detach the volume, run a filesystem check, and reattach.

## Abyss dead-letter topic
Helios Pipe routes messages that fail processing five times to a dead-letter topic named Abyss, which an engineer reviews daily.

## Account Cancellation
Customers can cancel anytime from the dashboard. Cancellation stops future billing but does not refund the current period unless the 30-day refund policy applies. Data is retained for 30 days after cancellation, then deleted.
